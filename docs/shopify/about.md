### Traditional Chinese (primary)

JapJapVan 把日本藥妝店會賣、溫哥華貨架不一定有的日常，送到你家門口。

店名拆開是 Japan、Japan、Vancouver：日日，溫市。護膚、頭髮、開架彩妝、和風文具。不是精品專櫃，也不是行李箱代購。

我們在香港松本清和萬寧按條碼買。每件出貨前拍盒正面、JAN、批號。你付的是那一支，不是倉裡不知道哪一年的貨。

你先預訂，我們才買本週的清單。貨每週從香港集運到溫哥華。預購成功到簽收，多數在 10 至 21 日。標價是加幣到戶價：運費、關稅、GST 已算進去。不應該再收到第二張清關帳單。

我們不賣未獲加拿大衛生部授權的化學防曬，也不賣眼藥水和口服美白。商店跟旅客自用不是同一條路。

japjapvan.com 由香港 Forest Charm Limited 營運。結帳店名是 JapJapVan。

### English

JapJapVan ships Japanese drugstore staples that Vancouver shelves often skip: lotions, essences, hair masks, colour cosmetics, stationery.

The name is Japan, Japan, Vancouver. Everyday Japan, for this city. Not a prestige counter, and not a suitcase reseller.

We buy at Matsukiyo and Mannings in Hong Kong, against the barcode. Before your parcel leaves, we photograph the box face, JAN, and lot. You get that piece, not a random year from a warehouse.

You pay first. We buy that week’s list. Parcels leave Hong Kong once a week. Most orders arrive 10 to 21 days after you check out. The CAD price is landed: shipping, duty, and GST are in the sticker. You should not get a second customs bill.

We do not sell chemical sunscreens that Health Canada has not authorised, or eye drops, or oral whitening. A shop is not personal luggage.

japjapvan.com is operated by Forest Charm Limited (Hong Kong). The checkout name is JapJapVan.
